Kazuki

walk
hold to run; keep going and he sprints
jump; hold for height
again in the air: somersault
crouch and crawl; at a run, curl up and roll for as long as you hold it
over the hat: pick it up
hold, aim with up and down, let go: throw the hat
hold by a crate: grab, then push or pull
hold by Kizuki: carry the cat. Tap: cat off your back
look, talk, carry and throw small things
beside Kizuki: stroke the cat
have a word with the sun
on a ledge: climb up
on a ledge: swing; jump lets go
in water: swim fast
in water: dive
switch to Kizuki

Kizuki

walk
hold to run, then gallop
jump; hold for height
against a wall: spring off it
hold against a wall in the air: claws in
while clinging: climb. On the ground: stand up
hold on the ground: gather; let go to pounce
the full pounce
hold in the air over Kazuki: ride on his back
crouch; from a crouch, jump is a pounce
at a run: curl up and roll downhill
hold: puff up
meow, bat small things, jump up at people
over the hat: carry it to Kazuki
switch to Kazuki
hold: stand up. Some things copy that
in the dark: sniff
