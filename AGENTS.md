# Project architecture

- The home page’s major visual bands remain in normal document flow without timed entrance transforms, so scrolling never reveals artificial gaps.
- The home page’s content section and every banner inside it carry the `bg-ink-black` backing, and images that sit just below the hero are preloaded and decoded on mount — no light container colour may ever show through a band while scrolling.
- The About intro uses clipped copies of its original transparent handwriting artwork for a brisk line-by-line reveal synchronized to the hero background decode; reduced motion shows it immediately.
- The Comics opening keeps phones on their original sequence with the God of Lies cover as its feature image; tablet and desktop reveal only a decoded priority-loaded double-height Seoul panorama, with portrait/landscape framing isolated by orientation.
- Keep the tablet/desktop panorama free of coloured masks; animate brightness on the background image alone during the pan so its foreground remains undimmed. Preserve temple/balloon artwork and the original phone sequence.
- Overlay controls that sit translucent on a panel's edge must never rely on a CSS `border` on that panel, because the line shows through them; the Young Adult slideshow draws its frame as separate edge segments with a gap where the arrow tabs sit, so keep the panel border-less and extend those segments if the tabs change size.
- The tablet/desktop Comics opening peel becomes available as soon as the underlying panorama decodes, uses opaque paper and a permanently transparent hotspot, and signals reveal progress to start the trees and title before completion; its clipped cue and wheel/swipe use the same turn without mapping live video to a mesh.
- The lower Comics panorama uses a fixed four-picture gallery above lower-centred story copy; predecode its images and fade the gallery as one composited layer with tight text shadows, avoiding costly stacked shadow kernels and local coloured halos. Its accessible enlargement dialog animates between the picture slot and the expanded image without reflow, and the image itself is the dismissal control.
- The Comics peel overlay is visually hidden whenever the opening page is not shown, because accessibility hiding alone leaves the reflected flap visible after return scrolling.
- The title-screen characters, dog and trees share the opening reveal/title-section state so trees reverse their fold while the title, characters and dog fade on departure; and measured space beneath the title maintains bottom anchoring and proportional dog placement without overlap or phone changes.
- Rebind character sizing measurements whenever the pinned Comics pages return from Pendragon, because those pages unmount and their replacement elements otherwise lose the measured sizes.

- A tap on the tablet/desktop God of Lies title half advances through the existing section transition; never intercept taps in an image dialog.
- Keep the title auto-advance consumed flag on the Comics page mount, not the pinned scene, so returning from Pendragon or the opening cannot restart the one-time timer; schedule only after the first settled title arrival.
- Tablet/desktop lower content lives in a looping Embla three-screen slideshow with a persistent footer and vertically centred arrows; keep its mounted scene inside the lower panorama half to preserve the opening pan and cutout measurements without vertical unlocks.
- Synchronize each lower slideshow arrow's clipped dark layer to the moving paper slide's actual bounds on Embla scroll events; defer screen React updates until settle to avoid layout work during travel. The footer is light only on the paper shelf screen and switches on Embla select (the moment an arrow is tapped), never waiting for settle; instant jumps (scrollTo with jump) never emit settle, so always sync screen state manually after them. The arrows fade out on press and back in as soon as the track is within a few pixels of its target snap — never on settle, which lags the visual landing by ~700ms and leaves the arrows hidden over an already-stationary slide.
- Pinned Comics pages must stay returnable: touch drag-up steps back like the wheel, and on real mouse pointers only, the cursor entering a thin strip just below the fixed header steps back one page; neither may fire while a dialog is open or from touches/pointers in the header, so header links always navigate.
- Keep the lower slideshow arrows mounted inside the lower panorama half with its content, without section-based hiding or entrance opacity, so their position relative to the pictures and blurb stays constant throughout the vertical pan. Clear leftover travel state when `open` changes; preserve horizontal arrow-press fades and per-arrow paper clipping.
- The shelf uses a portalled accessible unboxed reader with cover-to-slot zoom animation, isolating focus and gestures from the page navigation while showing story copy beside the cover.
