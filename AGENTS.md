# Project architecture

- The home page’s major visual bands remain in normal document flow without timed entrance transforms, so scrolling never reveals artificial gaps.
- The home page’s content section and every banner inside it carry the `bg-ink-black` backing, and images that sit just below the hero are preloaded and decoded on mount — no light container colour may ever show through a band while scrolling.
- The About intro uses clipped copies of its original transparent handwriting artwork for a brisk line-by-line reveal synchronized to the hero background decode; reduced motion shows it immediately.
- The Comics opening keeps phones on their original sequence with the God of Lies cover as its feature image; tablet and desktop assemble trees and the shrine-with-stairway around the title over one preloaded double-height Seoul panorama that pans from sky to hill road (where the blurb sits, replacing the cream page), with portrait/landscape framing isolated by orientation and a sky-blue fallback.
- The shrine stairway is sized per orientation so the first viewport edge cuts across its upper stair landing; its lower flight is aggressively masked away before the blurb screen, and the preserved balloon artwork is not currently rendered.
